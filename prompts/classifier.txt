CORRESPONDENCE - Letter, email, notice, memo
QUOTE_REQUEST - Request for quotation, tender, ITT
APPLICATION - Payment application, valuation, interim cert
REPORT - Progress report, survey, inspection record
SAFETY - Risk assessment, method statement, RAMS, COSHH
HR - CV, job application, training request, timesheet
PHOTO - Site photo, progress photo
OTHER - Anything that doesn't fit the above

Respond with ONLY a JSON object:
{
  "classification": "INVOICE",
  "confidence": 0.95,
  "summary": "Supplier invoice from ABC Plumbing for bathroom installation, £2,450 + VAT",
